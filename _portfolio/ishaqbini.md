We undertook a reconnaissance of the existing water dristributin network from a borehole located on the banks of River Tana. We then conducted a topographic survey of several proposed pipeline routes to serve the community drinking water, livetock and wildlife within a sanctuary. We also sized the required pumps and storage tanks to provide at least 1 month of storage if supply is disrupted.
